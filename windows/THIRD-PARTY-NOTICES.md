# Third-party notices

## Noto Sans SC

The settings window embeds a subset of Noto Sans SC so Chinese and English text
render without a language pack. Noto Sans SC is licensed under the SIL Open
Font License. The full license is in `third-party/NotoSansSC/OFL.txt`.

## OpenCV Haar cascades and YuNet

Eye state still uses the frontal-face, eye, and eyeglasses cascades from
OpenCV (`data/haarcascades`). GPU face detection uses the YuNet ONNX model
from the OpenCV Zoo (`face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx`). OpenCV and the
OpenCV Zoo models are licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
See https://github.com/opencv/opencv and https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo.

## OpenCvSharp

Camera capture and cascade detection go through OpenCvSharp, also Apache-2.0.
See https://github.com/shimat/opencvsharp.
